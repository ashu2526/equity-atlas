Model integrity

Valuation includes both Advent share issuance and capital proceeds, avoiding dilution-only modelling.

Formula: (forecast EBITDA × exit EV/EBITDA − net debt + unspent equity proceeds) ÷ diluted shares. Assumed net debt ₹39.2 Cr. Deal completion, ESOPs, timing, lease treatment, and proceeds spent on acquisitions may materially change the result.

Do not count deal-funded acquired earnings in EBITDA and simultaneously leave all deal proceeds as cash. This is a sensitivity model, not an investable price target.
