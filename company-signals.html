How to interpret these observations

Short-term breakouts

Compare the latest full-session close against previous 20- and 55-session highs, with relative volume versus the preceding 20 full sessions. Require follow-through and distinguish corporate-action distortions.

Long-run reversals

Use 20-, 50- and 200-session moving-average context. A reclaim or loss of the 200-day mean is a watch, not a forecast or an entry instruction.

Accumulation / distribution

Chaikin Money Flow (20) and a signed-volume OBV proxy can flag divergences between price and traded-share volume. Neither proves which institution bought the shares.

Fundamentals before action

Check company filings, sector relative strength, delivery data, earnings revisions and valuation before treating any chart pattern as decision-grade evidence. Missing or stale data suppress active signals.
