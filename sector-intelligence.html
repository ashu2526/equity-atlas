Investment Opportunity

Evidence gate: No company receives a numeric score until we verify comparable ROIC, FCF, segment economics, governance, a full valuation and the market's forward earnings expectations. The initial rank in the older Sector Radar is a manual research-priority order, not output of this score model.

How the future ranking will be calculated

Quality score

Weighted fundamentals and qualitative industry advantage (0–100) with source and confidence for each factor.

Opportunity score

Risk-adjusted, valuation-aware upside and expectation gap (0–100); independently calculated.

Coverage gate

When required evidence is missing, show 'Unrated' rather than invent a score or use market capitalisation as a proxy.
