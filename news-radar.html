Regime framework

1. News-pressure shifts

Do independently reported stories increase, decrease or persist across equal date windows? Track source diversity and missing coverage.

2. Economic transmission

Translate each event into direct and second-order sector beneficiaries, downside exposure and an explicit invalidation condition.

3. Market confirmation

Before calling a regime change, check credit spreads, commodity prices, rates, earnings revisions, sector-relative returns and breadth. Some data are not yet connected.

Research review rules

Each fortnightly analysis must recheck source articles, distinguish the archived news sample from published market facts, cite primary information where possible, compare conclusions with the prior report and make falsifiable tailwind/risk hypotheses. The initial 11 Nov report is provisional. Scheduled ChatGPT automation and GitHub upload are separately monitored: if a scheduled review fails to publish, the previous report stays visible with its original date.

Data architecture

Daily automated discovery

Scheduled GitHub Action checks selected publisher and institutional RSS feeds, then tags headline links by disclosed sector/macro keywords. Feed failures and missing coverage are recorded.

Permanent snapshots

Each scan records its date, successful topics, sampled counts and missing coverage. Raw article links are deduplicated and kept for a limited rolling archive; snapshot summaries persist longer.

Source-controlled narratives

Verified event and exposure hypotheses live in a separate curated ledger and Google Sheet. A headline cannot silently upgrade a thesis to an investable opportunity.
